Overview

Terskel is a running training app. It is designed to keep your data on your device. This policy describes what information the app can access, how it is used, and what we do not do.

By using Terskel, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Information the app processes

1. Information you provide in the app

When you set up training or use the app, you may enter:

  • Training background (for example experience, recent volume, consistency)
  • Goals (race distance, optional race date and target time)
  • Optional recent race results
  • Schedule preferences (run days, optional strength days, facilities)
  • Session feedback (for example whether a run felt controlled, effort notes, pain notes)
  • Manual run logs (duration and related fields you enter)
  • Unit preferences (miles or kilometres)

This information is used only to build and adapt your training plan and to show your progress on your device.

2. Health and fitness data (optional)

If you grant permission, Terskel may read selected Apple Health / HealthKit data to improve plan adaptation, such as:

  • Workouts and related workout metrics
  • Heart rate data associated with runs
  • Route or distance context when available
  • Active energy when available

Terskel is intended not to request sleep, cycle tracking, nutrition, body measurements, or clinical health records as part of its core training product. Permission screens in the app describe the categories we request.

You can refuse Health access. The app still works with manual logging.

Health data, when authorized:

  • Is used on device to match runs to planned sessions, estimate control/effort context, and adapt training
  • Is not uploaded to a Terskel backend (there is none for training data)
  • Remains under your control via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health / Terskel

3. Device and system data

The app may use standard platform features such as:

  • Local storage (preferences and training state on device)
  • Workout scheduling (for example system workout reminders when you enable them)
  • Apple Watch connectivity for running a planned session on Watch

Diagnostic crash reports may be available to us through Apple if you have opted in to share app analytics with developers via iOS system settings. That is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not a separate Terskel analytics SDK.

4. TestFlight and App Store Connect

If you join a beta via TestFlight, Apple processes your tester email and feedback under Apple’s terms. Feedback you send to our feedback email is handled as described under “How to contact us.”

How we use information

We use on-device and optional Health data only to:

  • Generate and update your training plan
  • Show today / plan / progress screens
  • Record and review sessions
  • Improve how the app adapts to what you actually ran

We do not use your training data to build advertising profiles.

What we do not do

  • We do not require you to create a Terskel account
  • We do not operate a Terskel cloud service that stores your runs or plan as of this policy date
  • We do not sell personal information
  • We do not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or injury prevention guarantees
  • We do not intentionally collect data from children for commercial profiles (see Children’s privacy)

Data storage and retention

Training data is stored locally on your devices. If you delete the app, local app data is removed according to iOS behavior. Data you chose to write into Apple Health remains in Health until you delete it there.

You can clear or reset in-app training state using any reset or data-clear controls offered in the app (for example demo/reset tools in development builds).

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only if:

  • You direct it (for example you export or share something yourself)
  • Required by law (valid legal process)
  • With service providers that process limited operational data on our behalf in the future — only if the product changes; this policy will be updated first
  • Apple processes distribution, payments (if any), TestFlight, and HealthKit under Apple’s own privacy policies

Security

We rely on Apple platform security for on-device storage and HealthKit permissions. No method of storage is perfectly secure; keep your device passcode/biometrics enabled.

Children’s privacy

Terskel is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

International users

The app is designed for on-device use. If you use Terskel outside the United States, your data still primarily remains on your device. Contact us with region-specific questions.

Medical disclaimer

Terskel provides coaching guidance for running training, not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting or changing a training program, especially if you have injuries, pain, or medical conditions. Stop and seek care if you experience pain that changes your stride or other concerning symptoms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
